ABOUT THE PLACE

Discover Antargange

Antargange is a beautiful natural destination near Kolar, known for its rocky hills, fascinating caves and scenic surroundings. Its unique granite formations make it a wonderful place for nature lovers and explorers.

Visitors can explore the rocky terrain, enjoy trekking and experience panoramic views of the surrounding landscape. The area is also known for its spiritual significance and the temple located nearby.
