TRADITIONAL KARNATAKA CUISINE

Karnataka Oota

A wholesome traditional Karnataka meal bringing together rice, sambar, rasam, vegetables, curd and delicious accompaniments.

Karnataka Oota is more than just a meal — it is a complete South Indian dining experience. A traditional serving can bring together rice, sambar, rasam, palya, sagu, curd, papad, chapati, sweets and other seasonal preparations. In Kolar, different restaurants offer their own style of this comforting and satisfying meal.

KOLAR FOOD CULTURE

More Than Just a Meal

Karnataka Oota reflects the everyday food culture of Karnataka — simple ingredients, comforting flavours and a variety of dishes served together. From rice and sambar to vegetable palya, curd and sweets, every part of the meal adds something different to the experience.
