KARNATAKA TRADITIONAL FOOD

Ragi Mudde

A traditional Karnataka staple made from nutritious finger millet flour and water.

Ragi Mudde is one of Karnataka's most loved traditional foods. Soft, wholesome and filling, it is commonly served with sambar, saaru, curry or other flavorful accompaniments.
