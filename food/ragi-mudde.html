KARNATAKA CLASSIC

Ragi Mudde

A wholesome Karnataka staple made from ragi flour, traditionally enjoyed with flavorful saaru, sambar and local accompaniments.

Ragi Mudde, also known as Ragi Ball, is prepared by cooking ragi flour with water and shaping it into soft, smooth balls. It remains a familiar part of Karnataka's traditional food culture.
