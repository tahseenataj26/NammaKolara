KARNATAKA TRADITIONAL FOOD

Akki Roti

A traditional Karnataka rice-flour flatbread known for its soft texture, crisp edges and simple, comforting flavour.

Akki Roti is a popular Karnataka dish prepared mainly with rice flour and traditionally cooked on a hot tawa. It is enjoyed as a wholesome meal with chutneys, curries and other accompaniments.

🍚
MAIN INGREDIENT RICE FLOUR
